Frequently Asked Questions

Everything students usually ask before requesting access.

A student-led technical community established for the Cybersecurity Department under the Institution of Engineers (India) — a platform to learn, collaborate, build projects, organize events and connect classroom concepts with real-world engineering.

✓ ANSWERED // 01

Every student — any branch, any year, zero experience required. Membership is open to all you need is curiosity.

No. Workshops and labs start from the fundamentals — from OSINT basics to malware reverse engineering — and progress to advanced tracks once you're comfortable.

✓ ANSWERED // 03

Hands-on workshops, live-fire red vs blue drills, hackathons like Grid Con and the Cyber Defense Hackathon, threat-intel sessions, forensics and OSINT labs, plus research and project building.
