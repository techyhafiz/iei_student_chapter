// 01 — ABOUT

The campus
firewall.

iei@cybersec:~

The IEI Student Chapter is a student-led technical community established for the Cybersecurity Department of our college under the Institution of Engineers (India).

We act as a platform where students can learn, collaborate, build projects, participate in technical activities, organize events, and connect classroom concepts with real-world engineering and cybersecurity applications.

Our goal is to promote technical learning, encourage exploration of cybersecurity, provide practical exposure, and build a strong technical culture within the Cybersecurity Department.
